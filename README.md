## Overview
**Supernatural** is an American fantasy/horror drama television series following two brothers as they hunt supernatural creatures, unravel cosmic threats, and face family legacy. The show blends horror, drama, humor, and long-running mythology across its multi-season arc.

---

## Quick Facts
- **Creator:** Eric Kripke  
- **Original run:** September 13, 2005 – November 19, 2020  
- **Seasons:** 15  
- **Episodes:** 327  
- **Original networks:** The WB (early seasons) → The CW  
- **Genre:** Fantasy, Horror, Drama, Adventure

## Premise / Synopsis
After the tragic death of their mother, Sam and Dean Winchester travel the country in a 1967 Chevrolet Impala hunting demons, ghosts, monsters, and other supernatural threats. As the brothers solve weekly cases, they uncover larger supernatural conspiracies, family secrets, and cosmic battles that escalate over the series’ run.

---

## Seasons & Episodes Summary
- **Season count:** 15  
- **Episode count:** 327 total episodes  
Each season advances both standalone "case-of-the-week" stories and a season- or series-long mythology arc. Later seasons expand into angels, demons, cosmic beings, and alternate universes while revisiting themes of family and destiny.

---

## Themes & Tone
- **Family & Brotherhood:** the emotional core is the relationship between Sam and Dean.  
- **Fate vs Free Will:** recurring tension about destiny, prophecy, and choice.  
- **Mythology:** lore-mixing from folk tales, religious texts, and original cosmology.  
- **Tone:** shifts between horror, drama, road-trip buddy comedy, and tragic epic.

---

## Why Watch
- Strong central performances and chemistry between leads.  
- Long-form storytelling with emotional callbacks and character development.  
- A mix of standalone monster-hunting fun and serialized mythology for long-term viewers.  
- A passionate fan community and cultural impact (memes, fanfic, conventions).
