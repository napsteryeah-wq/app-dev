# Supernatural

GOATED TV series **Supernatural**.

## About the Show
Supernatural is an American TV show that started in **2005** and ended in **2020**.  
It has **15 seasons** and **327 episodes**.  
The show is about two brothers, **Sam and Dean Winchester**, who hunt monsters, demons, and ghosts.

## Main Characters
- Sam Winchester (Jared Padalecki)  
- Dean Winchester (Jensen Ackles)  
- Castiel (Misha Collins)

## Creator
The show was created by **Eric Kripke**.

## Genre
Fantasy / Horror / Drama

## Why I Like It
I like Supernatural because it has cool stories about monsters, angels, and family.  
The brothers always stick together no matter what happens.

## Fun Fact
Their car is a black **1967 Chevrolet Impala**. It's called *Baby* in the show.
